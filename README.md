#  Global Corporate Ecosystem Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes the **Global Corporate Ecosystem** by comparing **IT and Non-IT companies** across multiple performance indicators such as ecosystem strength, leadership resilience, workforce risk, and market competitiveness.

The goal of this project is to identify patterns in corporate performance, employee attrition, and ecosystem maturity across different countries and industries.

---

##  Project Objectives

* Analyze the **Global Corporate Ecosystem Index (GCEI)** across countries
* Compare **IT vs Non-IT industries**
* Evaluate **employee attrition and workforce risk**
* Identify **top companies by leadership resilience**
* Examine **market competitiveness and revenue performance**

## Dashboard Sections

### 1️ Global Corporate Overview

* Country-wise corporate market competitiveness
* Global GCEI country rankings
* Industry distribution (IT vs Non-IT)
* Attrition comparison by industry

### 2️ Workforce & Risk Analysis

* Workforce risk matrix
* Employee risk levels (High / Medium / Low)
* Attrition vs employee count analysis
* Company workforce competitiveness

### 3️ Corporate Ecosystem Index (GCEI) Analysis

* Ecosystem strength by industry
* Ecosystem distribution categories
* Top companies by leadership resilience
* Lowest performing companies

### 4️ Company Insights & Performance Analysis

* Company-level dataset
* Market competitiveness vs revenue matrix
* Risk category analysis by company

### 5️ Leadership Strength Matrix

* Corporate leadership benchmarking
* GCEI vs GLRI score analysis
* Company leadership comparison

### 6️ Country-Level Industry Split

* IT vs Non-IT company distribution
* Country filters
* Company ranking by employee count

##  Key Insights

* IT companies slightly dominate the dataset with **509 companies** compared to **491 Non-IT companies**.
* Countries such as **USA and China lead in corporate ecosystem performance**.
* Higher workforce size tends to correlate with **higher attrition risk**.
* Several companies show strong **leadership resilience despite moderate ecosystem scores**.
